In a world where the Star Ring has shattered, choose from 10 heroes in a 5v5 MOBA for iPhone. Team up with four AI allies, push three lanes and destroy the enemy Star Core.
Builds, vision, boss fights and teamfights: a full MOBA in matches of roughly 10 to 18 minutes.
A Tutorial and Practice mode help newcomers. Matches against AI need no connection, and you can also play online with friends.

◆ HOW TO PLAY
- Pick a position (Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot or Support) and push the lane with your minions
- Destroy the outer, inner and base towers of each lane in order; once one base tower falls, the Star Core can be attacked
- Defeat the jungle's Azure and Crimson Sentinels and the river's Astral Wyrm and Ancient Colossus to swing the match
- Level up to 15 and power up your Skills and Ultimate
- AI allies and enemies hold lanes, clear the jungle, rotate, retreat, fight for bosses and team up. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard

◆ NEW TO MOBAS? START HERE
- Tutorial: five chapters, about five minutes; offered at first launch, skippable, and replayable any time
- Practice: try every hero with Infinite Gold and No Cooldowns
- Easy controls: the Attack button auto-targets nearby enemies, and skills cast with a tap (auto-aim) or a drag (manual aim). Auto-level Skills and Recommended Items are on from the start

◆ 10 HEROES, 5 ROLES
Duelist, Ranger, Arcanist, Support and Assassin, two heroes each. Every hero has a Passive, two Skills and an Ultimate. All 10 heroes are available from the start; heroes are never unlocked or sold.

◆ 92 ITEMS, 15 BATTLE SPELLS, 7 EMBLEMS
- Items: attack, magic, defense and movement; buy anywhere, any time, and combine at a discount using the components you own. Boots can carry a jungle or roam blessing
- Battle Spells: Blink, Cleanse, Ignite and more
- Emblems: seven sets with freely combined talents

◆ MODES
- Classic (Standard): you and four AI allies vs five AI enemies
- Ranked: an AI ladder with snake-order hero picks and seven tiers, Meteorite to Star Sovereign; the AI toughens as you climb
- Brawl: single lane, no jungle, faster pace; earns rewards but no rank points
- Rising: a win-to-advance run against AI
- AI & Custom: pick your side, AI strength and map
- Auto Battler: you vs seven AI rivals
- Spectate and Replays: watch AI vs AI (no rewards or records), or rewatch your auto-saved matches at 0.5x to 8x with rewind and file sharing

◆ PLAY ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
- Share a room code, or join players on the same Wi-Fi: 5v5 for up to 10 players, with AI in any empty seats
- Add friends with friend codes and invite them to your party. The host sets the teams and spectator seats
- No free-text chat. Block or report players; hosts can remove players from the room
- Requires accepting the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service; not available to players under 13

◆ COSMETIC-FOCUSED PURCHASES
- Free to play. Optional in-app purchases: AstralGem (shown as "Astral Gems" on the purchase sheet) and Star Pass Premium. Spend AstralGem on cosmetics such as recall and spawn effects, emotes, avatar frames and kill effects
- Cosmetics don't affect combat, nothing paid is randomized, and every hero is available to everyone; heroes are never sold
- Star Pass: 30 levels, free and premium tracks. Premium is purchased each season and adds AstralGem and other rewards on top of cosmetics

◆ BUILT FOR PHONES
- Landscape touch controls, left-handed layout, Colorblind Mode
- No ads, no account or login; pause any time during a MOBA match

◆ NOTES
- Minors need a parent's or guardian's consent to buy. Monthly spending limits apply by age (15 and under: ¥5,000; 16 to 19: ¥10,000; 20 and over: no limit).
- Matches against AI work offline. Online matches, friends and purchases need a connection.
- Game data stays on your device and is deleted with the app. AstralGem balances lost through device loss, reset or app deletion can't be restored.
- Requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later; landscape only.
