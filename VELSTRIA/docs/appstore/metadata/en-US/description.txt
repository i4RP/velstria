The Star Ring has shattered. Across the broken world of Velstria, 24 heroes clash over three lanes for the lost shards of the Star Core.

VELSIA: Star Ring Arena is a full 5v5 MOBA built for iPhone. Team up with four AI allies against a five-hero AI squad, push your lanes alongside waves of minions, break through the enemy towers, and destroy their Star Core to claim victory.

No connection required. Play on the train, on a plane, anywhere — every match takes about 10 to 18 minutes and is packed with the mind games and teamwork of a true MOBA.

◆ 24 HEROES, 6 ROLES
Vanguard, Duelist, Ranger, Arcanist, Support and Assassin. Every hero has a passive, three skills and an ultimate. Start teamfights, strike from the shadows, rain damage from the backline or keep your team alive — each role changes the flow of battle.

◆ A REAL 3-LANE MAP WITH A JUNGLE
Endless minion waves, three layers of towers, the Azure and Crimson Guardians, and two river bosses: the Astral Wyrm and the Ancient Colossus. Use vision and brush to set up ambushes, and fight over the bosses to swing the game.

◆ 72 ITEMS, 10 BATTLE SPELLS, 30 RUNES
Shop at any time during a match and upgrade components into powerful items. Combine battle spells like Blink, Cleanse and Ignite with rune pages to create your own build for every hero.

◆ AI THAT THINKS
Allies and enemies read the game: laning, jungling, retreating, recalling, contesting bosses and grouping for teamfights. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard.

◆ MODES FOR EVERY MOOD
- Standard: pick a difficulty and battle the AI
- Ranked: draft with bans and climb seven tiers from Meteorite to Star Sovereign
- Practice Range and Tutorial: try any skill or item freely
- Spectate: watch AI teams battle it out
- Replays: relive and study your own matches

◆ FAIR BY DESIGN
Paid items are cosmetic only — skins, emotes, recall effects and more — plus the Star Pass premium rewards. Heroes are unlocked with Starlight Coins earned by playing. You can never buy combat power, and there are no randomized paid items.

◆ ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
Daily missions, the Star Pass with free and premium tracks, match history, achievements and saved replays.

◆ BUILT FOR EVERYONE
Touch controls designed for landscape play, indicators that never rely on color alone, and a full Japanese and English interface.

◆ NOTES
- Free to play with optional in-app purchases (Astral Gems and Star Pass Premium).
- Minors should obtain a parent's or guardian's permission before purchasing. Monthly spending limits apply based on the age group you select.
- Your game data is stored on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.
- Requires an iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
