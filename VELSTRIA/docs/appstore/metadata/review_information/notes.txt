VELSTRIA: Star Ring Arena is a fully offline 5v5 MOBA. Every teammate and opponent is computer-controlled (AI). There is no server, no account or login, no chat, no multiplayer and no user-generated content. No network connection is needed to play; only In-App Purchases use Apple's StoreKit.

FIRST LAUNCH
1. Accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
2. Select an age group. Please choose "20 or older". Younger age groups have monthly purchase limits (under 16: JPY 5,000 per month, 16-19: JPY 10,000 per month) that can block test purchases.
3. Enter any player name. The tutorial can be played or skipped.

WHERE TO FIND EACH FEATURE
- Play a match: Home > Play > Standard (vs AI, Easy / Normal / Hard) or Ranked (vs AI, draft with bans) > choose a hero > confirm the loadout. A match takes about 10-18 minutes.
- To see a short session, use Home > Play > Practice. Practice can be left at any time from the in-match menu.
- Tutorial: Settings > Tutorial.
- Spectate an AI vs AI match: Home > Play > Spectate. Replays of your own matches: Profile > Replays.
- Heroes, items, runes and battle spells: from the Home screen menu.
- Language: Settings > Language > English (by default the app follows the device language).
- Delete all local data: Settings > Privacy.
- Contact / support: Settings > Support.

IN-APP PURCHASES
- 6 consumable Astral Gem packs: com.velstria.game.gem.60, .gem.300, .gem.980, .gem.1980, .gem.3280, .gem.6480
- 1 non-consumable: com.velstria.game.pass.premium (Star Pass Premium, Season 1)
Steps: Home > Store > Currency Store > tap a gem pack > confirm. The paid gem balance increases as soon as the verified transaction is finished. Gems are spent on cosmetic items in the Store. Star Pass Premium: Home > Star Pass > Unlock Premium. Restore Purchases is available in the Store.

Paid items are cosmetic only and never change combat stats. There are no randomized paid items (no loot boxes): every product shows exactly what you receive. Heroes are unlocked with Starlight Coins that are earned by playing, not with real money.

PRIVACY
The app does not collect any data. The profile, replays and purchase records are stored only on the device. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The app uses no encryption other than what iOS provides.

DEVICE
iPhone only, landscape orientation, iOS 18 or later.
