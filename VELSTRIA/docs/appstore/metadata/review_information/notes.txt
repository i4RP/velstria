VELSTRIA: Star Ring Arena is a fully offline 5v5 MOBA. Every teammate and opponent is computer-controlled (AI). There is no server, no account or login, no chat, no multiplayer and no user-generated content. No network connection is needed to play; only In-App Purchases use Apple's StoreKit.

FIRST LAUNCH
1. Tap to start, then select an age group. Please choose "20 or older". Younger age groups have monthly purchase limits (15 and under: JPY 5,000 per month, 16-19: JPY 10,000 per month) that can block test purchases.
2. Accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
3. Enter any player name. First-time setup runs entirely on the device (no download). The tutorial can be played or skipped.

WHERE TO FIND EACH FEATURE
- Play a match: Home > BATTLE > Standard (vs AI, choose Easy / Normal / Hard) > Choose Hero > Confirm > DEPLOY. Ranked (vs AI, draft with bans): Home > BATTLE > Ranked > Start Draft. A match takes about 10-18 minutes.
- For a short session: Home > Practice. A practice match can be left at any time from the in-match menu.
- Tutorial: Home > BATTLE > More > Tutorial (also Settings > Support > Tutorial).
- Spectate an AI vs AI match: Home > Spectate. Replays of your own matches: Home > Replays.
- Heroes, items and runes: Home > Heroes / Items / Runes. Battle spells and rune pages are chosen on the Choose Hero screen before each match.
- Language: Settings > Language > English (by default the app follows the device language).
- Delete all local data: Settings > Privacy.
- Contact / support, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy: Settings > Support.

IN-APP PURCHASES
- 6 consumable Astral Gem packs: com.bitcoinpay.velstria.gem.60, .gem.300, .gem.980, .gem.1980, .gem.3280, .gem.6480
- 1 non-consumable: com.bitcoinpay.velstria.pass.premium (Star Pass Premium, Season 1)
Steps: Home > Store > Get Gems (or tap the currency counter at the top of Home) > tap a gem pack > confirm. The paid gem balance (including the pack bonus) increases as soon as the verified transaction is finished. Gems are spent on cosmetic items in the Store. Star Pass Premium: Home > Star Pass > Premium. Restore Purchases: Home > Store > Restore (also on the Star Pass premium panel).

Paid items are cosmetic only and never change combat stats. There are no randomized paid items (no loot boxes): every product shows exactly what you receive. Heroes are unlocked with Starlight Coins that are earned by playing, not with real money.

PRIVACY
The app does not collect any data. The profile, replays and purchase records are stored only on the device. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The app uses no encryption other than what iOS provides.

DEVICE
iPhone only, landscape orientation, iOS 18 or later.
