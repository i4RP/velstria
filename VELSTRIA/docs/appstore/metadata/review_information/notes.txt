VELSIA: Star Ring Arena is a 5v5 MOBA for iPhone. All single-player modes (AI matches, Practice, Tutorial, spectating, replays) run on the device with no connection. The app also offers optional online play: one player's iPhone hosts a room, and others join with a 6-character room code (through our relay server on Cloudflare Workers) or from Nearby rooms on the same Wi-Fi (Local Network/Bonjour). There is no account or login, no free-text chat and no voice chat. In-App Purchases use Apple's StoreKit.

FIRST LAUNCH
1. Tap to start and choose "20 or older". Younger groups have purchase limits that may block test purchases; under-13 players can't use online features.
2. Accept the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.
3. Enter any player name (offensive words and contact details are rejected). The tutorial can be played or skipped.

WHERE TO FIND EACH FEATURE
- Match vs AI: Home > BATTLE > Standard > Choose Hero > Confirm > DEPLOY. Ranked: Home > BATTLE > Ranked > Start Draft. About 10-18 minutes.
- Short session: Home > Practice.
- Tutorial: Home > BATTLE > More > Tutorial.
- Spectate: Home > Spectate. Replays: Home > Replays (.vreplay files hold match setup, inputs and names; shared only via the share sheet).
- Data deletion, blocked players, Stop Using Online Features: Settings > Privacy. Support and policies: Settings > Support.

ONLINE PLAY (a match needs two iPhones)
1. Device A: Home > Online. The first time, read the Online Rules, check "I agree to the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service" and tap "Agree and Continue". A party opens with a room code; "Invite Players" shares it. iOS asks for Local Network access here.
2. Device B: Home > Online > agree > Join by Room Code > enter the code > Join (or pick the room under Nearby on the same Wi-Fi).
3. Device A: Start Game. AI plays any empty seats.
4. Friends: each device shows "Your friend code". On B, Add a friend > enter A's code > Send; on A, Friend Requests > Accept. Friends can then be invited from the friend list.
One device is enough to check the Online Rules, room code, friend code and safety settings. If you need a second player, contact us via the review contact details and we will join from our device.

SAFETY (Guideline 1.2)
- Filter: display names and room names are checked on the device when entered and when received.
- Report: "..." next to a player (party slots, participant list, friends, requests, invitations) > Report > reason > a draft email to info@btcpay.jp with the player's name, friend code and player ID. After an online match: results screen > Report.
- Block: same menu > Block. Their requests and invitations are discarded and they can't join rooms you host. Unblock in Settings > Privacy.
- Hosts can Remove from Room. Party invitations are accepted only from friends.
- We begin reviewing reports within 2 business days and can suspend a violator's friend code.

PRIVACY
Our relay keeps only friend data: friend codes, names in requests held for absent players (14 days), and each inbox's key hash and last connection time. Delete Friend Server Data or Delete All Data (Settings > Privacy) erases it. Room traffic is forwarded in real time, not stored or logged. No analytics, ad or tracking SDKs; no ATT prompt. Relay traffic uses iOS TLS; same-Wi-Fi traffic is plain TCP. No proprietary encryption.

DEVICE
iPhone only, landscape, iOS 18 or later.
