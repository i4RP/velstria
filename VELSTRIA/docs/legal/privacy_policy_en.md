This Privacy Policy explains how {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the iPhone game
"VELSTRIA: Star Ring Arena" (the "App").

## 1. Summary

The App is an offline game. We do not operate any servers, and **we do not collect personal information or any other data through the App**.
The App does not track you, does not show advertising and does not include analytics.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To run the game, the App stores the following information **only on your device**. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

| Information | Details | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Player name, owned items and currencies, game progress, settings | Providing the game |
| Age group | The age group you select (we do not ask for your date of birth) | Applying monthly purchase limits for minors |
| Purchase records | In-App Purchase transaction IDs and the items granted | Delivering purchases, preventing duplicate grants, restoring purchases |
| Replays | Input records of your matches | Replay playback |

## 3. In-App Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple Inc. through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment method or Apple Account details.
When a purchase completes, the App receives a verified transaction from Apple on your device and uses it only to deliver the purchased item.
Please see Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for how Apple handles your information.

## 4. Sharing and third parties

Because we do not collect data through the App, we do not sell, share or disclose it to third parties.
The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not use App Tracking Transparency.
If you have enabled "Share With App Developers" in iOS, Apple may provide us with crash reports (diagnostic data that does not identify you), which we use only to fix bugs.

## 5. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything else you choose to include.
We use this information only to respond to you and to investigate the issue you report, and we delete it once it is no longer needed.
We do not disclose it to third parties unless required by law.

## 6. Security and deletion

- Data on your device is protected by iOS Data Protection.
- You can delete all data stored by the App from Settings > Privacy in the App. Deleting the App also deletes this data.
- We cannot recover deleted data. You can export a backup from the App beforehand; you choose where the exported file is saved.

## 7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
Minors should use the App and make purchases only with the permission of a parent or guardian. The App applies monthly purchase limits based on the selected age group.
If a child has emailed us, a parent or guardian may ask us to delete that correspondence.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information.
Because the App does not collect personal information, these rights apply only to correspondence you have sent us; contact us to exercise them.
We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when laws change or when we add features (for example, online features).
If a change expands the information we collect or how we use it, we will notify you in the App or on our website in advance and obtain consent where required.
